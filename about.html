Meet The Founder

Kabelo Maseko

Barista · Entrepreneur · Visionary

Kabelo Maseko is the heart and soul behind Morda Café. Born and raised in Johannesburg, Kabelo built Morda Café with one mission — to create a space where every cup tells a story and every visit feels like home.

With a deep passion for craft coffee, wholesome food, and genuine hospitality, Kabelo opened Morda Café in 2023, bringing a warm modern café experience to the vibrant heart of Sandton. He personally selects every blend, oversees every recipe, and ensures every guest leaves satisfied.

From introducing handcrafted boba tea and cold-pressed smoothies to curating a breakfast menu that speaks to every palate, Kabelo's vision goes far beyond coffee. Morda Café is his love letter to Johannesburg.

"A great café isn't just about great coffee. It's about the moments you create over it."

A Café Made for Everyday Moments

Morda Café is built around comfort, flavour, and quality. From your morning coffee to a slow breakfast, a quick pastry, a refreshing smoothie, or a handcrafted boba — every item is made to bring warmth to your day.
